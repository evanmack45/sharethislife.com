Privacy, consent, and refunds

This page is for the story chapter. It covers consent, what we do with a recording, and how refunds work.

Consent

Before you can pay, you confirm this:

The person speaking knows they’re being recorded and agreed to it being turned into a chapter.

If that isn’t true, don’t send us a recording. If we learn it wasn’t, we won’t write the chapter, and we’ll refund you.

You must be 18 or older.

What we do with the recording

You email us an audio file, or a share link, from the address you used at checkout. We use it only to write and revise your chapter.

  • We delete the audio and the transcript 30 days after we send the chapter.
  • We don’t clone anyone’s voice.
  • We don’t reuse a recording for any other chapter, product, or training.
  • We don’t share it, except as needed to process the order (for example, payment through Stripe).

What goes in the chapter

Every quote comes from the recording. We don’t invent words. The chapter is drafted with AI and checked line by line. If we’re unsure of a name or a date, we send those back to you as questions instead of guessing. You get one free revision.

Refunds

Full refund on request. Write to us at sharethislife@agentmail.to from the email you used at checkout.
