What to record

Ten to twenty minutes is enough. Ask them first, sit somewhere quiet, and use the voice memo app already on the phone. One story, or a stretch of ordinary life, works better than trying to cover everything.

How it works

  1. Record a conversation

    Ten to twenty minutes with a parent or grandparent, or a recording you already have. Ask them first. An order includes a one-page card with phone tips and ten good questions.

  2. Send the audio

    When orders open, you email the file to sharethislife@agentmail.to from the email you used at checkout, and include the speaker’s first name and relationship. If the file is large, send a Google Drive, Dropbox, or iCloud share link instead.

  3. We write the chapter from their words

    The chapter is drafted with AI and checked line by line. We don’t invent quotes. Names and dates we can’t confirm come back to you as questions.

  4. You get it back within 48 hours

    A PDF and an editable text file, in the same email thread, with one free revision if you want a change.
