Twenty free questions

A short interview you can ask yourself, or use when you sit down to record. Nothing you type here is sent to us — this page is just the questions.

Childhood

Ordinary days, and the ones that stuck.

  1. What did an ordinary Saturday look like when you were about ten?
  2. Who was the grown-up you most wanted to sit next to, and what did you talk about?
  3. Was there a place you went when you needed to be alone? What was it like there?
  4. What food takes you straight back to being a kid, and who usually made it?

Family

The people you came from, and the ones you made a life with.

  1. What do you wish people knew about your parents that doesn’t come through in photographs?
  2. Which family habit or saying has stuck with you, even if you rolled your eyes at it then?
  3. Who in the family could always make a hard day easier, and how did they do it?

Love

Partners, friends, and the small things that still matter.

  1. How did you know you were falling for someone — a particular moment, or a slow change?
  2. What is something small a partner, a friend, or a family member did that you still think about?

Work

Jobs, pride, and the days that never made it onto a résumé.

  1. What was your first real job, and what do you remember about the first week?
  2. Was there work you were proud of that never showed up on a résumé? What was it?

Hard times

The heavy years, and what got you through them.

  1. When life got heavy, who did you call, or where did you go?
  2. Is there a hard year you came through that changed how you treat other people?

Lessons

What you believed, what you changed, and what you’d tell a younger relative.

  1. What did you believe at twenty-five that you see differently now?
  2. What’s a piece of advice you were given that turned out to be right — or completely wrong?
  3. If a younger relative asked how to be a decent person, what would you actually tell them?

Wishes

What you hope we keep, and what you’d still like to do.

  1. Is there a story about your life you hope someone in the family will keep telling?
  2. What do you hope people feel when they leave your house?
  3. If we could spend one ordinary day together from any year of your life, which day would you pick?
  4. Is there something you’ve never been asked that you wish someone would ask you now?
